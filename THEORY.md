## Why Epistemic AI?

In high-stakes domains, "Common Sense" or "Probabilistic Weights" are insufficient. We require **Justified Belief**.

### 1. The Uncertainty Principle
AI should not hallucinate certainty. Trignum treats every request as a measurement. If the evidence agreement is low, the system acknowledges the uncertainty and requests human intervention.

### 2. Temporal Validity
Authorization is not a snapshot; it's a dynamic state. Evidence that was valid 5 minutes ago (e.g., "Physician Present in OR") may no longer be valid now. Trignum's Tensor RAG includes a time dimension to ensure reality-alignment.

### 3. Provenance and Chain of Custody
In multi-agent systems, intent is often diluted across hops. We preserve the "Epistemic Identity" of the original human intent through every delegation, ensuring that the final action is always tethered to an authorized source.
